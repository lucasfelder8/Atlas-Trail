      How to play %GAME_NAME%

      1. Find today's hidden destination country. You start from the green country.
      2. Tap any country touching your green region, then press Guess to add it. Or type its name in the search box and press Enter twice: once to select it, once to guess.
      3. Every guess unlocks a new hint about the destination. The last hint is a famous landmark.
      4. Tap a country to compare it with the hints: ✓ it shares that fact with the destination, ✗ it doesn't.
      5. An orange banner and outline appear when the destination touches one of your countries. The dotted lines on the map are the equator and the tropics.
      6. Stuck on a hint? Tap 💡 Map beside it to see every country that fits. It's optional: each hint you look up adds a 💡 to your shared result.
      7. Fewer guesses is better. Par is the shortest possible route. Three new puzzles every midnight (Pacific time): Easy (par 3–4), Medium (5–6) and Hard (7–8), each with its own streak.
      8. Want to study? Explore lets you tap any country to see its language, religion, currency and more.
