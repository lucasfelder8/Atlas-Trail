    How to play %GAME_NAME%

    1. Find today's hidden destination country. You start from the green country.
    2. Tap any country touching your green region, then press Guess to add it.
    3. Every guess unlocks a new hint about the destination. The last hint is a famous landmark.
    4. Tap a country to compare it with the hints: ✓ it shares that fact with the destination, ✗ it doesn't.
    5. An orange banner and outline appear when the destination touches one of your countries. The dotted lines on the map are the equator and the tropics.
    6. Fewer guesses is better. Par is the shortest possible route. A new puzzle every midnight (Pacific time).
